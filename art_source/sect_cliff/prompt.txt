Seamless horizontally tileable texture for a 2.5D isometric action game: the stone retaining wall and cliff face under the edge of the floating ruined sect courtyard, as in the attached concept boards (the platform stands on cliffs above the mist). Seen straight from the front, flat orthographic, no perspective. Top band: a row of large dressed purple-grey stone blocks with a worn capping course; below it the masonry gives way to rough dark purple-grey rock with cracks, hanging olive moss, ivy and a few red maple leaves; the bottom third darkens and fades into misty violet shadow. Detailed hand-painted game art matching the references, soft dusk light from above.

The left and right edges must tile seamlessly. No props, no lanterns, no doors, no waterfalls, no characters, no text. Landscape 1536 x 512.
