## model sheet (attached to every request below)

Character model sheet of the Yin Corpse (阴尸), an undead cultivator corpse exactly as on the attached reference board: gaunt grey-white skin, long tangled black hair with a loose topknot, a yellow paper talisman strip with red glyphs across the forehead and more yellow talisman strips hanging from the robe, tattered layered slate-blue and grey Taoist robes with frayed edges, a dark red sash, long bony clawed hands, bare feet with dark wraps. hunched forward, arms hanging forward with clawed hands.

Style: hand-painted dark xianxia game sprite, clean dark ink outline about 3 px, painted cel shading lit softly and neutrally from straight above (no directional key light, no rim light, no glow, no cast shadow, no smoke, no aura), 3-4 large tone masses, readable at small size.

Camera: orthographic isometric game camera looking down at 30 degrees above the horizon (top of the head and the back visible, the body slightly foreshortened), the same camera in every frame.

Layout: one row of four full-body poses on a 1536 x 1024 canvas, same scale, feet on the same baseline, evenly spaced, nothing overlapping, from left to right: 1 facing the viewer (south, front view), 2 facing down-right (south-east, three-quarter front), 3 facing right (east, side view in profile), 4 facing up-right (north-east, three-quarter back).

Background: perfectly flat solid pure green #00FF00 everywhere, no floor, no shadow, no gradient, no effects. No text, no labels, no numbers, no watermark.
